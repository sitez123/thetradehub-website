# The Trade Hub website: notes

Style: Floaty Bright. Built from data.js, so edit text, products and FAQs there.

## Placeholders to fill in data.js
- company.phone, company.whatsapp, company.address, company.licence (email set: sales@thetradehubuae.com)

## Design decisions
- Palette taken from the tiles: onyx white, café noir (Glamour Black Café), lagoon (Tech-Marble teal niche),
  zellige green (glazed bricks), calacatta gold, copper vein, travertine, grey silk, oak.
- Type: Instrument Serif (display), Hanken Grotesk (body), JetBrains Mono (figures).
- Floating objects: real tile textures cropped from RAK photos, plus tile-spacer crosses.
- Signature piece: a container that fills by weight as you scroll (20ft / 40ft / Jebel Ali pallet).

## Assets
- Photos: RAK Ceramics (rakceramics.com, onlineshop.rakceramics.com), used with the owner's stated permission.
- Amani, Panda, Medicea and Urban Marble product images are illustrations (labelled on the page). Replace with RAK photos.
